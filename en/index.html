About Van Zoest

Van Zoest Vastgoed is a private real estate company based in Rotterdam, founded in 2020 by Wilfred van Zoest. We invest in real estate throughout the Netherlands and hold it for the long term.

Our portfolio is broad. We invest in apartments and houses, student housing, large sites where companies can house migrant workers, housing for young people, and care properties for the elderly and other care groups.

Wilfred van Zoest has a background in construction. That shapes how we look at buildings: solid, careful and with attention to good maintenance. We buy buildings to keep them, and we make sure they are in good shape for tenants and users.

Our organisation

Letting and management: Vastor

Our real estate is let and managed by our own management company, Vastor Verhuur & Beheer, founded in 2026 by Wilfred van Zoest and Bo van Wijngaarden, who own it together. From then on we had enough real estate together to bring letting and management fully in-house, so we keep the quality of management in our own hands.
